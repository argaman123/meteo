The lead intentionally lets me do changes he could trivially make himself, **so that I keep taking notes of his feedback and write it right the first time next session** (2026-06-23: "im letting you do the work even when its easy for me to change, because I want you to keep taking notes of the feedback i give you to write it better next time… make sure every time you take the right notes and keep doing that all the time even after context is cleared").

**Why:** he is actively teaching his style and does not want to repeat himself; the value of each correction is lost unless it's persisted. Context clears, so chat-only memory is useless to him.

**How to apply:** treat every design correction as a durable lesson, not a one-off edit. After any feedback that reveals a preference, update [[code-design-taste]] (or the relevant note) — capture the *principle*, with a concrete example and his wording. Do this continuously, every session, without being reminded. When applying his taste up front, you should rarely be correcting the same thing twice. Related: [[design-first-concise]], [[concise-ask-dont-choose]].

**Timing (his correction, 2026-07-07):** save the note only once he has approved the result — not mid-iteration. Recording a lesson while the fix is still unvalidated reads as "telling yourself it's good without confirming" (his words, after I saved a note during the contour-label back-and-forth). Iterate first; when he confirms the result, persist the lesson that turn.
